## grassland-01-16x9.png

Create one original production game background image, 16:9 wide landscape, for a beautifully illustrated monster-catching RPG battlefield. Entirely original hand-painted ink-and-watercolor anime environment, clean delicate contour lines, softly textured gouache, luminous natural greens, restrained warm sunlight. No existing game assets or copied compositions. Scene: tranquil rolling grassland clearing bordered by meadow grasses and small wildflowers; distant layered rounded hills and woodland, spacious pale blue sky, natural environmental depth. Camera is low, slightly above a flat battle arena ground plane, looking toward a horizon at 40 percent image height. Lower 60 percent is spacious continuous gently textured grass floor, foreground close and opponent area farther back. Keep the foreground lower-left and midground upper-right ground unobstructed for separately rendered creatures. Ground is continuous: absolutely no oval battle platforms, no mounds, no circular patches, no creatures, no people, no shadows of missing characters, no text, no HUD, no borders. Compose detailed scenery at far sides and horizon, quieter playable ground. This must be a finished illustration filling the entire rectangular image, not a mockup, not a collage.

## mound-grass-01.png

Create one standalone transparent game asset: an irregular low oval patch of short lush meadow grass and earthy turf to place beneath a creature in a hand-painted monster-catching RPG battle. Only the independent mound asset on genuine transparent background. Wide shallow ellipse, roughly 3:1 width to height, seen at a low battle-camera angle slightly above the floor. Delicate ink outlines and softly textured watercolor/gouache anime illustration, yellow-green grass lit by gentle daylight, subtle earth visible at a very low broken edge, a few tiny white and yellow wildflowers at the outer sides. Keep center flat, clear, unobstructed for creature feet. Soft wispy irregular grass edges blend naturally into surrounding grass scenery, avoid hard black outline or thick raised island. Do not include scenery, creatures, people, text, UI, ground plane outside the oval, or cast shadow beyond the mound. Frame the entire single mound with transparent padding around all edges, no cropping. Original polished artwork.
