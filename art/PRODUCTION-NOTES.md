# Original artwork production

Day and night are separately generated illustrations with matching geography. Runtime day/night tinting is disabled. Independent night mounds are paired with night backgrounds. Weather atmosphere remains a separate scenery layer.

The first grassland depicts a lake valley; variation two depicts an upland pasture with an old stone wall and hawthorn. These must remain distinct scenery compositions rather than flips or palette swaps. Each receives its own metadata and dedicated aspect compositions. New source candidates are not counted as calibrated or release-ready until final validation.

Required collection remains three to five distinct variations for every environment in DEVELOPMENT-SPECIFICATION.md, all nine aspect families, and Mobile/HD/Ultra-HD resolution tiers. Existing prototype sources do not satisfy that full collection.

Tests and rendered validation are deferred to the end by user instruction. Original generation output is preserved without Python image manipulation. Exact generation prompts are stored under prompts/.
