# Slate gorge — landscape aspect batch

Eight original paintings provide independent day/night artwork for rocky terrain variation 2 in 4:3, 16:10, 3:2 and 21:9. Built-in image generation was used. Unchanged originals remain in art/source/rocky_terrain-02-*.png; the exact prompts are in art/prompts with matching names.

Visual review found broad level dry slate gravel, distant angular cliff walls and clear standing regions, without creatures, UI, water, tiles or baked-in mounds. Separate gravel day/night mounds are registered. Source dimensions:

- 4x3: day 1448×1086, night 1448×1086
- 16x10: day 1586×992, night 1586×992
- 3x2: day 1536×1024, night 1536×1024
- 21x9: day 1916×821, night 1916×821

Day sources retain full framing. Night source regions begin at y120 except 3:2 at y160, with centered exact-aspect widths. These regions raise distant cliff bases and exclude the moon completely rather than cutting through it. Horizon and contact settings are drafts pending the combined native review.

No tests, packaging or publication occurred. Native sprite geometry and battle UI remain untouched. The subsequent tall/superwide batch supplies the remaining variation 2 families; variation 3 still needs its non-widescreen sets.
