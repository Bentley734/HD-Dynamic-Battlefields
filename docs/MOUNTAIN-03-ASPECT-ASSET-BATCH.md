# Mountain variation 3 — remaining display families

Sixteen independent day/night paintings extend the terracotta plateau to all nine display families. Built-in image generation produced the unchanged originals in art/source; exact prompts remain in art/prompts. Visual review found broad dry gravel standing areas, distant layered mesas and sparse juniper, without baked-in battlers, mounds or UI. Separate terracotta day/night mound materials are selected.

Landscape source dimensions are 1448×1086, 1586×992, 1536×1024 and 1916×821. Night regions start at y160 for 4:3 and 3:2, and y120 for 21:9, excluding the moon entirely where cropped. Both 32:9 sources are 2115×743; full-width exact-ratio regions start at y40 by day and y140 at night. Portrait sources are 940×1672/941×1672 for 9:16, 928×1695/909×1731 for 9:19.5, and 793×1983 for 9:20. Exact-ratio framing corrects generated aspect mismatches. The 9:20 day region starts at y0 to retain the sky; night starts at y220 to exclude the moon without clipping it.

Horizon and contact settings remain authoring drafts awaiting the combined native review. No tests, packaging or publication occurred. Battle UI and native sprite geometry are unchanged. All additional location paintings remain preserved for future use.
