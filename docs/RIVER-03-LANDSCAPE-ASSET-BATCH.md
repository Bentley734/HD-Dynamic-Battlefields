# River variation 3 — landscape families

Eight original independent day/night paintings for 4:3, 16:10, 3:2 and 21:9 were generated using the built-in image generator. Exact prompts and unchanged originals are saved in art/prompts and art/source. The reed-lined summer river and distant arched footbridge distinguish this variation from the gorge; clear water occupies the battler standing zones, with no baked-in islets or UI.

Local registrations select independent sand-islet day/night materials and shared water grounding metadata. Initial horizons are .20, .21, .20 and .23 respectively. Source-art visual review is complete for this batch, but native alignment, sizing and compatibility remain untested. No tests, packaging or publication were performed. Extra location artwork is retained.
