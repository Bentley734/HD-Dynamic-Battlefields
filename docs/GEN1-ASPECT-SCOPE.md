# Gen 1 remaining aspect artwork scope

Latest user instruction: only create remaining aspect variations for Gen 1 areas with trainer battles or wild Pokemon. Defer areas without battles. Preserve every existing source, including non-battle location artwork, for possible future use.

This supersedes the earlier request to finish all non-battle locations now. It does not remove existing backgrounds or implement random trainer encounters. It does not cancel the finished Gen 1 release, final validation, separate sprite offsets, quality packs or other functional requirements.

Before scheduling another environment, inspect actual Gen 1 encounter/trainer/static-battle sources. Treat scripted rival, legendary and other static battles as battle locations. Water encounters count. Service interiors, shops, ordinary residential interiors, elevators and decorative roofs must not receive more aspect artwork without evidence of a native battle. Do not infer battle eligibility solely from environment names or existing map classifications.

Current active deep-cave variation 2 remains in scope for native cave encounters. Completed city/village and other non-battle source artwork is retained. Deferred aspect gaps must be distinguished from missing battle-location artwork in release records.

2026-10-16 beta priority: after coastline variation 3, author at least one complete nine-aspect variation per Gen1 battle area, then validate and publish a beta. Defer additional variations until after that beta; preserve them and the broader objective. See GEN1-BETA-MILESTONE.md.
