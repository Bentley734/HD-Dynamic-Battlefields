# Ocean first variation: ultrawide

Original 21:9 day/night paintings made with built-in ImageGen. Unchanged sources saved as `art/gen1/21x9/ocean/ocean-01-21x9.png` and `art/gen1/21x9/ocean/ocean-01-21x9-night.png`; exact prompts retained under `art/prompts` with matching basenames.

Both visually inspected for open standing areas, high horizon, wide island skyline and no baked mounds. Registered with dedicated 21:9 metadata and independent grass-islet day/night materials. No renderer or map-rule changes.

Remaining ocean display families and variation counterparts are unfinished. Native grounding, blending and viewport behavior remain untested. No tests, packaging or publication performed. Battle UI, sprite defaults and Alpha 6 mound sizing unchanged.
