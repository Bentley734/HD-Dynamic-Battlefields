# HD Dynamic Battlefields — 0.3.0-beta.6

Mounds now follow the artwork actually selected by **1025Dex 1.5.2**, including DBK Animated, Gen9 Static, BW ROM, EE Animated, EE Static and KIM sprites. This update changes HD Dynamic Battlefields only. It does not edit 1025Dex, its artwork, Pokémon positions or battle UI.

- Corrects transparent padding in Pack alignment, including the separate back-side metrics used by front-facing and mirrored player sprites.
- Grounds each doubles battler's mound independently. Keeps intentional hovering and animation motion.
- Preserves your mound offsets and scale settings, material alignment and water-islet calibration.
- Uses the displayed Pokémon during Gen 2 queued switches. Pending/native substitute and ghost pictures retain their existing placement.
- Includes the previously reviewed native Hoenn Metagross and Salamence opponent mound adjustments.

Install **HD-Dynamic-Battlefields-0.3.0-beta.6-base.zip**, replacing the previous mod folder, then restart the game. **Existing artwork downloads remain compatible; no artwork redownload is needed.** Requires gen1recomp **0.3.95**.

Audited all **1,389 registered species/forms**, all six source choices and their female/shiny fallbacks. Original-sheet and production-layout coverage is exhaustive; real battle and anatomical visual reviews are targeted. See [the validation report](https://github.com/Bentley734/HD-Dynamic-Battlefields/blob/main/docs/DEX-FOOTING.md) for exact scope and results. Physical Android performance and full battle playthroughs were not exercised by these desktop fixtures.

KIM checks cover its public sprite provider with its separate battle stage disabled. Unknown future provider versions safely retain existing mound placement until recalibrated. Your own manual mound or sprite offsets can intentionally change the calibrated footing.
