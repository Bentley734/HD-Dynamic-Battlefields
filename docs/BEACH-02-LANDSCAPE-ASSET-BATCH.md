# Beach variation 2 — landscape compositions

Eight original day/night paintings for 4:3, 16:10, 3:2 and 21:9 were made with the built-in image generator. Exact prompts and unchanged source images are retained in art/prompts and art/source. The sheltered dune lagoon is visually separate from the volcanic cove, with dry ivory sand at both standing zones and no baked-in battlers or mounds.

Night source regions raise the shoreline while preserving aspect: 4:3 x66.6667 y100 width1314.6667 height986; 16:10 x79.4 y100 width1427.2 height892; 3:2 x120 y160 width1296 height864; 21:9 x140.1667 y120 width1635.6667 height701. These are draft composition settings, not proof of native grounding. Independent sand night materials are selected.

No tests, packaging, publication or changes to battle UI/native sprite geometry were performed. Extra location sources remain preserved.
