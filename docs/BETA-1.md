# Gen 1 Beta 1

Release candidate status: six artwork aspect releases verified; remaining portraits uploading, main beta pending.

Coverage: 46 positively identified Gen 1 battle environment types have variation 1 in all nine supported artwork aspect families. Many natural environments additionally retain three complete variations. S.S. Anne trainer cabins use private cabin scenery and Gary uses corridor scenery. The former lounge paintings remain preserved for future use. Existing nonbattle paintings are retained; their missing aspect ratios remain deferred.

Distribution: lightweight base with a bundled original wide grassland day/night fallback, plus nine independent screen-ratio choices. Each ratio offers One Per Area and All Variations. All 18 actual ZIPs passed transactional offline installation, checksum, corrupt-file/path/index rejection, recovery and deterministic-build checks. The official v0.3.89 network facade also downloaded and verified the published 16:10 compact pack (95 files, 226,660,999 installed bytes) into an isolated memory cache.

Catalog integrity: 904 compositions, 1,279 composition periods, 1,331 decoded PNG assets. All 15 pure Lua checks passed. Official v0.3.89 isolated native fixture checked integer sprite corrections, reset, default UI preservation, and logical rendering at 1920x1080, 5120x1440, and 900x1600. Menu Customizer 3.35.4 isolated fixture passed 14 display sizes, day/night, variation and water/fishing batches. These fixtures do not prove complete gameplay traversal or compatibility with every sprite mod.

Pokemon X/Y controls are under Mounds > Player/Opponent, sharing global/aspect/current-background scopes and reset. Native drawBattlerPic corrections use whole logical pixels. Custom renderers bypassing that method need explicit provider integration. Battle HUDs, commands and dialogue remain owned by the runtime/UI mod.

Beta limitations: Red native fixtures are the current runtime evidence; Blue/Yellow traversal, full encounter transitions, physical mobile devices, expanded/animated sprite packs and custom renderer offsets remain unverified. Link presentation and Gen 2/3 adapters are not integrated. Three-to-five full variations everywhere, Mobile/Ultra-HD tiers, complete environmental animations and production asset distribution remain broader project work. This beta does not claim the complete specification is finished.

Full scope is preserved in DEVELOPMENT-SPECIFICATION.md. Do not enable KIM alongside this mod when investigating scenery bugs; simultaneous scenery ownership remains unvalidated.
